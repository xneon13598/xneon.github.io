About

XNEON OSINT is a small research project focused on open source intelligence — the practice of collecting and analysing information that is already publicly available: social media, public records, metadata, archived pages, and the countless small traces the internet leaves behind.

The goal is not surveillance. It's understanding what is already visible, and helping people understand what they themselves are sharing. Everything here is legal, ethical, and reversible.
